Methodology

How the system analyzes an image, stage by stage

◈ Error Level Analysis (ELA) LIVE

The image is resaved at a fixed JPEG quality; regions edited after the original compression re-compress differently, showing up as bright zones in the diff map.

  • Per-pixel RGB difference
  • Amplified heatmap rendering
  • Runs client-side

◉ Noise & JPEG Ghost LIVE

A high-pass residual isolates sensor/compression noise, while resaving at a second quality level reveals a "ghost" of prior compression — both flag spliced regions.

  • High-pass noise residual
  • Multi-quality resave diff
  • Complements ELA findings

▣ CNN Classification MODEL TRAINED CASIA v2.0

Convolutional Neural Network trained on the CASIA v2.0 dataset for Original vs. Tampered image classification.

  • Trained on CASIA v2.0
  • 4-Block Keras CNN Architecture
  • Classifies Original vs. Tampered
